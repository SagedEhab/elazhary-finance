# ELAZHARY FINANCE

Personal finance tracker for one person in Egypt, taken from the owner's own brief.

- **Job:** follow certificates and loans across banks with exact numbers, and see at a glance whether the portfolio earns more than it loses.
- **Use:** mainly on a phone, checked often, in Arabic or English. Should feel like the banking app the owner uses daily.
- **Tone:** cool and confident, but simple. Numbers come first.
- **Surface mode:** Operate.
- **Data:** stays on the device. No accounts, no server.
