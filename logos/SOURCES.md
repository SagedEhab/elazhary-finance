# Logo sources

Downloaded 2026-11-04 from Wikimedia Commons, English Wikipedia and Arabic
Wikipedia, then cropped to each bank's symbol. Some of these files are used on
Wikipedia as non-free logos, and all of them are the banks' trademarks. They're
here to identify the owner's own banks in a personal app, not for reuse.
